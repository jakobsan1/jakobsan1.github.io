Evaluating the return on investment of using Predictive Maintenance in a manufacturing facility

What is Predictive Maintenance?

Predictive Maintenance (PdM) is a proactive approach to equipment maintenance that leverages real-time data, machine learning, and predictive analytics to anticipate equipment failures before they occur. Unlike traditional maintenance strategies, which are based on fixed schedules or reactive responses, Predictive Maintenance optimises repair schedules, reduces costs, and minimises downtime by identifying potential issues before they escalate into failures.

How does Predictive Maintenance work?

Predictive Maintenance is data-driven, using real-time monitoring of machines to anticipate and prevent failures before they happen.

The process follows a structured workflow:

1. Establish Conditional Baselines: Define normal operating conditions for equipment (e.g. temperature, pressure, vibration, power wattage, oil levels)

2. Install Condition Monitoring Sensors: IoT sensors continuously track machine performance and detect anomalies.

3. Collect Conditional Data: Sensor data is sent to a centralised system or cloud for real-time analysis

4. Identify Baseline Breaches: Advanced algorithms detect deviations from normal conditions, predicting potential failures

5. Create Work Order: Once a fault is detected, a maintenance request is automatically generated

6. Perform Maintenance: Maintenance teams intervene before failure occurs, minimising downtime

7. Repeat PdM Cycle Continuous monitoring ensures ongoing optimisation and reliability

By predicting potential failures in advance, companies can schedule maintenance at the most optimal time. This approach is more efficient than reactive maintenance (fixing equipment only after it breaks) or preventive maintenance (servicing equipment at regular intervals regardless of its condition).
